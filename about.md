## About me
I'm an Analytics Manager with a background that spans data, software engineering, linguistics, and higher education. I care about building things that last — whether that's a data model, a team, or a learning culture.

I'm drawn to the messier, harder problems: legacy systems, data quality at the source, making analytics actually useful to the people who need it. Outside of work, I'm usually hiking, making something with my hands, or in the ocean.
